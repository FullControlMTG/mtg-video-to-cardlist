How to use MTG Card Scanner

Setup

  • Select your camera from the dropdown in the top bar.
  • Position the camera so that cards appear right-side up in the feed. The scanner reads the card name from the top-left corner, so orientation matters.

Scanning cards

  • Hold a card flat and face-up in front of the camera. A plain, contrasting background works best.
  • The scanner reads the top-left name strip of the card — make sure it is unobstructed and in focus.
  • Avoid glare on the card face. Tilting the card slightly away from the light source helps.
  • Detection works best when only one card is in frame at a time.
  • Recognised cards appear in the Detected This Session panel. Click + Add to add one to your decklist, or click the card image for more details.
  • If a card was mistakenly detected, click the × button to dismiss it. It can be re-detected on the next scan.

Building your decklist

  • Use the search bar to look up and add any card by name.
  • Switch between Main and Sideboard tabs to organise your list.
  • Click a card row in the decklist to open its detail view where you can adjust the quantity or zone.
  • Click the ⚙ icon to set a deck name, format, and notes.

Exporting

  • Use the export buttons at the bottom of the decklist to copy or download your list in Moxfield, MTGA, MTGO, or plain-text format.
